“We will never charge a single penny.”FRZ Generations policy. It is a principle, not a promise.

What free means

FRZ Generations does not charge money for any app, tool or service, now or later. We also do not restrict how much you use them.

How we keep it free

Our apps run on free API models and free third-party software. That is why we can offer them without a price.

What we cannot control

The companies behind those free API models set their own rules. They may limit usage and may collect the data you upload.

Questions people ask us

What is FRZ Generations?

FRZ Generations is a free-for-life family of education, school-software, games, support and library platforms built in Kerala, India. It was founded in 2025 and is owned and managed by its CEO, Faraz Ahammed CM.

Is anything on FRZ paid?

No. Every FRZ app and every FRZ Care service is free, and it will stay free for life. If anyone asks you to pay in our name, do not pay and report it through FRZ Care.

How can FRZ stay free?

Our apps run on free API models and free third-party software, so we have no running costs to pass on to you. We do not restrict your usage.

Which platforms does FRZ Generations have?

FRZ Generations has four platforms: FRZ Edu (study tools), FRZ Games (browser games), FRZ Care (support and policies) and FRZ OS (the OS division). FRZ Public Library sits under FRZ Edu, and FRZ School Sync and the private 1HOS sit under FRZ OS.

Where can I find free study tools?

FRZ Edu has twelve free tools: flashcards, quizzes, a study timer, short notes, revision notes, question papers, answer keys, SSLC notes, an attendance register, Explain It, History Finder and printables. Open it at frzedu.netlify.app.

Where can I download free notes?

FRZ Public Library is the open archive of FRZ Notes. Each PDF is catalogued by serial number, title and class, and anyone can download it without an account. It sits under FRZ Edu. Browse it at publiclibrary-frz.netlify.app.

What is FRZ School Sync?

FRZ School Sync is a free ERP for schools that brings day-to-day school operations into one system. It is part of FRZ OS and launched on 17 September at the smart classroom of PMSAPTHSS Kakkove. Open it at schoolsync-frz.netlify.app.

Is my data safe?

All apps without AI are 100% privacy protected. AI-backed apps send what you upload to a free API provider, and that provider may collect it. Avoid uploading anything private to an AI-backed app.

Why does an AI-backed app sometimes stop working or slow down?

The API provider behind it may impose usage limits. Those limits are set by the provider, not by FRZ Generations. Try again a little later.

Who is responsible for AI answers?

FRZ Generations is not responsible for AI-backed apps or their output. Check anything important against your textbook or your teacher.

Can anyone use 1HOS?

No. 1HOS is private and is only for PMSAPTHSS Kakkove Humanities students and teachers. It has no public link, and access is given by the 1HOS team.

Where are the full terms and conditions?

All terms and conditions are published on FRZ Care, along with the Privacy Policy, Cookies Policy, Disclaimer and the Data and Content Removal policy.

How do I report a problem or ask for content to be removed?

Raise a complaint on FRZ Care and keep your complaint ID to track it. Our target is to reply within 24 to 48 hours. For removal requests, follow the Data and Content Removal page.

Who owns FRZ Generations?

All other ventures and branches are entirely owned and managed by the CEO, Faraz Ahammed CM. FRZ Games also has a Managing Director, and 1HOS is built with the 1HOS Development Team.
